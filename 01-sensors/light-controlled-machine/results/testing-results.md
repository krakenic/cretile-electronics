# Testing Results

## Activity 1: Change Direction

| Condition | Observed Result |
|---|---|
| Light intensity below threshold 10 | Machine rotated in one direction |
| Light intensity above threshold 10 | Machine rotated in the opposite direction |

## Activity 2: Start Machine

| Condition | Observed Result |
|---|---|
| Light sensor exposed to light | Motor started |
| Dim/no light | Motor stopped |

## Activity 3: Stop Machine

| Condition | Observed Result |
|---|---|
| Dim/no light | Motor rotated |
| Light directed at sensor | Motor stopped |

## Problems Encountered

- Checked the connections when the motor did not respond correctly.
- Adjusted the sensor position when the readings were inconsistent.
